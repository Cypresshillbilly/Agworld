## Asset rule

The actual transparent PNG master files are the only approved source for inserting AgWorld branding into the platform. Never rebuild a logo from a screenshot or preview.

The AW icon must retain its complete spherical lower curve and signature leaf loop. Never flatten or crop the sphere.
