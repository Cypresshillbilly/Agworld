## Functional framework (page 9)

1. Identity/access: users, roles, permissions, authentication and employee profiles.
2. Agricultural database: farms, boundaries, farmers, crops, livestock, infrastructure, drones and activity history.
3. Geographic engine: satellite maps, polygons, markers, clustering, search and progressive zoom.
4. Farm visualisation: database-driven crops, cattle, water, buildings, machinery, farmers and drones.
5. Gamification: missions, evidence, XP, stars, badges, levels, skills, rewards and leaderboards.
6. Sales: territory discovery, opportunities, client missions, proposals and progression.
7. Technician: training, virtual repair lab, real repair evidence, assessments and certification.
8. Reporting/admin: staff progress, farm coverage, drone penetration, opportunities and compliance.

The governing principle is to separate stored farm facts from zoom-dependent rendering, and to keep gamification rules configurable. The pictures are product concepts, not evidence of delivered software.
