# AgWorld Player Command — Version 2

Historical baseline. [Immersive Drawers V3](PLAYER_SCREEN_V3_BASELINE.md) supersedes the visibility, Enter AgWorld, sidebar landscape and aluminum-frame rules below.

Approved by the product owner on 12 September 2026. This explicitly updates the V1 geometry and component rules; the reference image continues to govern color, typography, photography and diagrams.

- Keep the navigation, player workspace, dominant map and floating Command Center arrangement.
- At a 1280px shell width, retain the 180px navigation and widen the player workspace from 285px to 350px. Scale both widths with the shell.
- Dashboard contains Player Profile, the player's Sales Funnel, a non-scrolling Current Mission card, and six advisors.
- Move the complete five-axis Skills Profile to the Profile route, alongside progression, badges and completed milestones.
- Current Mission uses a relevant picture on the left, its complete objective on the right, rewards below and an always-visible Start Mission action. Start opens the mission experience; it never directly awards a completed onboarding mission.
- Preserve the 10px gap below Player Profile, 14px above the advisor bay, and alignment of the advisor bay with the floating Command Center.
- Give the Command Center a restrained brushed-aluminum rim around a continuous dark teal display. Keep its title centered and status independently aligned right.
- Territory Stats starts closed, occupies only its 28px handle when collapsed, expands left, and remains above the Command Center.
- Enter AgWorld belongs in the map menu and becomes Player View while exploring the full map.
- Keep the existing logo master. Hide its embedded micro-taglines in the navigation presentation and display the readable approved tagline over farmland at the foot of the sidebar.
- System Administrator is the sixth, non-skill advisor. Its portrait and welcome voice appear on explicit selection. The five specialists remain Compliance, Sales, Product, Operations and Technical. Switching away stops the administrator's audio.

## Data and media

Player XP, level, chapter, next mission and achievements come from the authenticated progression record. Five skill scores derive from the canonical skill reward model; no sixth skill is added. Sales activity is filtered by the authenticated player's ID, deduplicated by farm, and matched against the current shared map relationship. It is recorded activity, not assigned leads, quotes, sales value, or all Company contacts. Empty, loading and failure states are distinct.

The new Administrator portrait is a provisional fictional game persona pending the owner's forthcoming photos. Portrait likeness and personality direction will be refined from explicitly supplied references. Current Safety Training artwork depicts field safety instruction and protective equipment. Welcome narration is a locally hosted male AI voice; dynamic mission guidance uses available browser speech voices and retains the written briefing if speech is unavailable.

## Validation

Check the returning-player path (level 2, 460 XP, three completions), onboarding questions, profile skills, player-specific sales isolation, zero and failure states, advisor selection and audio stop/pause/resume, map-mode return, collapsed Territory Stats, and full mission visibility at 1600×1000, 1280×900 and 1280×720.
