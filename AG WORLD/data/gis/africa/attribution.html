Country outlines: Natural Earth, public domain, 1:50m. Somaliland geometry is included in Somalia's country record. First administrative divisions: geoBoundaries gbOpen, release commit 9469f09. Runfola et al. (2020), geoBoundaries: A global database of political administrative boundaries.

Boundaries are simplified for gameplay and retain their published source dates below. They are not a cadastral or legal boundary service. Provincial detail is available for 48 countries; South Africa also uses municipal and town boundaries from the DLRRD spatial service. SADC membership defines the 16-country opening view.

Flags: flag-icons, MIT license. Boundary coordinates are simplified and rounded to four decimal places. Derived boundary files and the complete source metadata are available in this data index and the public source repository. Upstream source licenses, including ODbL where applicable, are retained for these datasets.
